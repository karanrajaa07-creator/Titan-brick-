Terms & Conditions of Commercial Supply

Last Updated: January 2026 • Commercial Division

1. Governing Framework

All quotations, purchase orders, material dispatches, and warehouse staging agreements executed with Titan Brick & Concrete Industries Inc. are governed exclusively by these commercial terms and the statutory engineering mandates of ASTM C90, ASTM C129, and local building codes.

2. Compressive Strength & Warranty

Titan certifies that all solid and hollow concrete masonry units meet or exceed the specified compressive rating noted on the batch dispatch ticket. Samples tested by an accredited independent laboratory within 28 days of steam curing showing strength failure under ASTM C90 guidelines qualify for immediate replacement of the entire affected batch at zero cost.

3. Jobsite Unloading & Transit Zero-Breakage Policy

Our flatbed knuckle-boom crane offloading service is provided subject to contractor maintaining adequate, level, and unobstructed ground bearing capacity for outrigger deployment. Chipped or fractured units exceeding the standard 2% handling threshold must be noted on the bill of lading and will be replenished on the next staging dispatch.

4. Payment, Credit & Net 30 Terms

Standard commercial orders are payable upon dispatch confirmation unless an active wholesale Net-30 credit agreement is in effect. Past due commercial balances accrue statutory interest at 1.5% per month.

5. Force Majeure & Severability

Neither party shall be liable for delivery delays arising from extreme acts of nature, severe quarry aggregate shortages, or localized road closures beyond reasonable commercial control.
