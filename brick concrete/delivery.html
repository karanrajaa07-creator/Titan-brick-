Fleet Operations

Engineered Delivery for Congested Urban & High-Load Sites

Most third-party freight carriers drop heavy concrete blocks curbside, forcing contractors to hire secondary forklifts or waste labor hand-unloading. Titan eliminates this bottleneck entirely.

Every flatbed in our delivery division is outfitted with high-reach Palfinger rear hydraulic knuckle-boom cranes. Our certified operators hoist 1.5-ton pallets over site barriers, placing them directly onto 2nd or 3rd floor scaffolding decks.
